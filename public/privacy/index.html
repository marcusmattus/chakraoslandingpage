Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how chakraOS ("chakraOS", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and shares information when you use the chakraOS mobile application (the "App") and the chakraOS website (together, the "Service").

The short version: your journal and your hand-tracking camera feed stay on your device. We collect only what we need to run your account and your subscription. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Information that stays on your device

Journal

Journal entries, intentions and the "field" readings derived from them are stored and processed locally on your device. We do not receive or store the contents of your journal on our servers.

Mudra hand tracking (camera)

When you use mudra practice, the App asks for camera access. Camera frames are analyzed in real time on your device to detect hand positions. Frames are not recorded, saved or sent to us or anyone else. You can turn off camera access at any time in your device's Settings; other features keep working.

Because this data stays on your device, it is included in your device backups (such as iCloud Backup) according to your own device settings, and it is deleted when you delete the App.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect:

  • Email sign-up: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Sign in with Apple: a unique user identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address.
  • Sign in with Google: a unique user identifier, your name, email address and profile picture URL as provided by Google.

Account authentication is provided by Supabase, which stores this information on our behalf.

Subscription information

When you start a trial or subscribe, Apple processes your payment. We do not receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous App user ID linked to your account, your purchase history (product, price, dates, renewal and trial status) and basic device information such as device model, OS version, App version, country and language.

Technical information

Like most online services, our service providers may automatically log technical information when the App connects to them, such as IP address, device type, operating system and timestamps, for security and reliability.

Website

If you join our waitlist or contact us through the website, we collect the email address and any message you send. The website does not use advertising trackers.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide Premium features, verify your subscription and restore purchases.
  • To respond to support requests and send service messages (for example about your account or changes to our terms).
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we process personal data on these bases: performance of a contract (providing your account and subscription), legitimate interests (keeping the Service secure and improving it, in ways that do not override your rights), legal obligation, and consent where we ask for it (for example camera access, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings).

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account information: for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep it longer by law.
  • Subscription records: as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • Technical logs: for a limited period for security and debugging.
  • On-device data: stays on your device until you delete it or the App.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the App's settings, or by emailing us. This deletes your account information from our systems. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage that in your device's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • Camera: turn camera access on or off at any time in your device's Settings.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability and objection: depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Email us to make a request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • Complaints: you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Health information

chakraOS is a reflective wellbeing tool, not a medical service, and does not request medical or health records. Journal content you write stays on your device as described in section 1.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email us at support@chakraos.app.
